
# UTM Connect | Product Development & Business Analysis

## Project Overview

UTM Connect is a student-focused campus event discovery app developed to address the challenge of finding university events scattered across social media, club accounts, and promotional materials.

Working collaboratively in a university project team, we developed and tested an app prototype designed to centralize event discovery and help students stay connected to academic, extracurricular, and social activities at the University of Toronto Mississauga.

**My role:** Project Management Lead 

**Project:** University of Toronto Mississauga | Digital Enterprise Management | 2024

## The Business Problem

Students often rely on multiple disconnected channels to discover campus events, making it difficult to find relevant opportunities and stay informed.

Our proposed solution was a centralized platform where students could discover activities and event organizers could share upcoming events.

## Product Strategy & MVP

We translated student and organizer needs into product requirements and prioritized the following features:

- **Event discovery:** A centralized feed displaying campus events and activities.
- **User onboarding:** Sign-up and login interfaces.
- **Event posting:** An interface for organizers to submit event details and media.
- **Search and filtering:** Functionality intended to help students find relevant events by category, date, and location.
- **Personalized experience:** Opportunities for user profiles and improved event navigation.

The MVP focused on core onboarding, event browsing, and event submission, while additional discovery features were explored through subsequent iterations.

## Usability Testing & Product Improvements

We evaluated the prototype using task-based usability testing to understand how effectively users could navigate the application.

### Testing Activities

Participants were asked to:

1. Sign up and log in.
2. Create and submit a campus event.
3. Search for events and apply filters.

We assessed navigation, clarity of interfaces, ease of completing tasks, and overall user experience.

### Key Learning Areas

- **Onboarding:** Evaluating the clarity of registration and login.
- **Event creation:** Understanding whether organizers could easily submit event information.
- **Event discovery:** Assessing navigation and the usability of search and filtering.
- **User experience:** Identifying opportunities to improve interface consistency and navigation.

Testing findings helped inform priorities for refining the prototype.

## Product Development & Technical Context

The project involved:

- Figma for interface design and wireframing.
- Thunkable for prototype development.
- Firebase for user authentication.
- Airtable for data storage.
- Usability testing and feedback analysis to guide product improvements.

The project was developed as an academic prototype rather than a publicly launched commercial application. Some planned features remained incomplete or required further refinement.

## Business Value

UTM Connect illustrates how product development can address a user problem through structured requirements, feature prioritization, prototyping, and iterative testing.

The proposed platform aimed to make campus event information easier to access while providing student organizations with a centralized way to promote activities.
